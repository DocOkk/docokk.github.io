About Vcare Opticals

For over 25 years, our clinic at 21 Florence Avenue, Razole, has been a trusted provider of friendly, dependable and expert eye care.

Our mission and promise

We help every patient achieve clear, comfortable vision through personalised service and the latest optometry technology.

Our promise is to deliver exceptional eye care with compassion, accuracy and dedication. We are passionate about educating patients so they understand their eye health and have the guidance they need for ongoing care.

Whether you have a vision concern, need new frames or simply want a check-up, our experienced staff are here to support you.

Which optometrist should I see?

  • Diabetes, high blood pressure or eye disease checksDr Alan Koya
  • Children’s eyesightDr Daniel Wilson or Dr Melissa Javali
  • Family eye care and myopia controlDr Melissa Javali
  • Glasses, frames and lensesSarah Ravie

Meet the team

Dr Melissa Javali

OD, Paediatric and Family Optometry

Dr Javali has over 15 years of clinical experience in family and paediatric eye care. After earning her Doctor of Optometry degree from a leading Australian optometry school, she completed further professional development in myopia control and vision therapy.

New to our team

Dr Daniel Wilson

BOptom, Paediatric Optometrist

We are pleased to welcome Dr Wilson, a qualified paediatric optometrist with extensive experience in children’s vision care. He focuses on early detection of short-sightedness, long-sightedness, eye coordination issues and digital eye strain. His friendly, patient-centred approach helps children feel comfortable and relaxed during eye examinations.

Dr Alan Koya

BOptom

With more than three decades in practice, Dr Koya is widely respected for his expertise in diagnostic optometry, ocular disease detection and vision rehabilitation. He often works with ophthalmologists and GPs to give holistic care, particularly for people with chronic conditions such as diabetes or hypertension.

Sarah Ravie

Optical Dispenser and Lens Specialist

Sarah is a highly skilled optical dispenser with over 12 years of hands-on experience helping clients choose eyewear that blends comfort, function and personal style.

Emiliano (Emi) Brooklin

Receptionist

As the first point of contact, Emi sets a warm, welcoming tone for every patient. With professional experience in customer relations and medical reception, he keeps daily operations running smoothly and makes sure every visitor receives prompt, respectful and helpful service.
