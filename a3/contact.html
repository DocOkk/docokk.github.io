Questions before your visit

Which appointment should I choose?

For a general check, choose an eye examination or the Annual Eye Checkup. For a child, choose a children’s eye examination. If you’re unsure, call us and we’ll help.

What should I bring?

Bring your current glasses or contact lenses, any previous prescription, and a list of changes you’ve noticed in your vision.

How do I get the 25% online discount?

Book through our online booking form. The discount applies to your next eye-care visit.

Can I see an optometrist without visiting the clinic?

Yes. For minor concerns and follow-ups, book a virtual consultation.
